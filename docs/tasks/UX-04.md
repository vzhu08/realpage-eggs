### Delivered (existing contracts)

1. **Portfolio changes.** The comparison keeps its three counts (definite, uncertain, conflict) and adds: a timeline of
   the dates the changed rules' records state, with the two compared dates marked and each date's precision kept
   (a month stays a month); summaries by legal municipality and by rule category that filter the detail; names in
   place of IDs (street address and legal municipality; rule title, citation, category); and a drill-down from source
   document to changed rule to impacted property to before/after evidence, with a second grouping by property.
   Actual, hypothetical, blocked, partial and unknown stay distinct; blocked counts show "—". Names are read from
   `GET /addresses`, `GET /rules/{id}`, `GET /sources/{id}`; a failed read keeps the ID and offers a retry. Editing the
   request while a comparison runs withdraws it, and a late response is discarded.
2. **Source disagreements.** A new view shows the conflicts the evaluator already flags for a property and date as
   two source-backed claims side by side: exact text and offsets, source authority and type, enactment/effective and
   retrieval dates, the fields that differ, why it is unresolved and the next step, all in the payload's own words.
   It covers two records of one provision and an unresolved interaction between two rules, and works for any
   jurisdiction. It has no preferred source, ordering of authority or score. Lookups and changed rows link to it.
3. **Consequential questions and remaining uncertainty.** Each question states how many results an answer can move
   and each hypothetical lists exactly which; after an answer, a result that stays unknown says why and that no
   property fact can close it. Remaining uncertainty is grouped (identical statements once), split into "a fact can
   close these" and "no answer can close these", names the kind of next step from `Uncertainty.kind`, shows the
   service's remedy, and sets aside items about rules that do not reach the property. Answer provenance stays visible.
4. **Working export.** (In the hosted demo-only preview, which cannot save files, the same content is shown on the
   page instead of downloaded.) One JSON file of the result on screen, with stored facts, request-local answers and history,
   evaluator results, quotes and source records, evidence checks, model-review status and remaining uncertainty kept
   apart, and "independent human review: not recorded". It states that it is not the PLAT-06 evidence package.
5. **Judge demo.** `frontend/docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`: the four-minute script with a real-data track (examples to be chosen
   from verified results; none are chosen or implied here) and a synthetic rehearsal track. Labeled backup:
   13 frames in `frontend/docs/demo-backup/` and a recording regenerated by `DEMO_BACKUP=1 npm run demo:backup`.
   Method-note visuals: `frontend/docs/method-note/`. 36 documentation screenshots in `frontend/docs/screenshots/`.
