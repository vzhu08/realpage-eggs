UX DEVELOPMENT FIXTURE. Fictional City of Larch Point, Zenith (ZZ). Not actual law.
Larch Point Ordinance DEV-03, section 2. Enacted September 9, 2026.
Beginning October 15, 2026, a landlord of a residential rental property may not charge an applicant a screening fee greater than $35.
Section 2 does not apply to an owner-occupied building containing four or fewer units.
